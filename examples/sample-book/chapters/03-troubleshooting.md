# Chapter 3 - Troubleshooting

## In one sentence

When a cup tastes wrong, change one thing at a time and taste again.

## How it works

1. **Sour and thin** means the water took too little. Grind finer, or brew a little longer.
2. **Bitter and dry** means it took too much. Grind coarser, or brew a little shorter.
3. **Flat** is usually the beans: too old, or ground too long ago.

Remember: write down what you changed, or you will never know which change helped.

## Rapid-fire drill, 4 questions

- **1. Sour cup, first fix?** Grind finer.
- **2. Bitter cup, first fix?** Grind coarser.
- **3. Flat cup, first fix?** Fresher beans.
- **4. How many things to change at once?** One.
